Tkinter Geometry Managers
Tkinter provides geometry managers to control how widgets are arranged inside a window. The three main geometry managers are:

1. pack() method
The pack() method places widgets in blocks. One can control their position and whether they expand to fill the available space.

2. grid() method
The grid() method arranges widgets in a grid of rows and columns. Widgets can span multiple rows or columns using rowspan and columnspan.

3. place() method
The place() method positions widgets at specific x and y coordinates. You can specify absolute or relative positions.
